# Changelog

## 2026.11.3

- Optional ModJS scripting: a `BarelyProspecting` global with events for panning, hammering, soaking, the crucible and the lure, read access to basins, crucibles, lures and pannable blocks, and calls to add, replace or remove drop rows, hammer, soak, melt, fuel and bait rules. See `docs/scripting.md`.
- Four rubble now craft one cobblestone, and four grit one gravel, so there is a way back to the vanilla stones.
- Messages are plain facts now, with no instructions: the tray, basin, crucible and lure messages lost their "do this next" sentences.

## 2026.11.2

All textures redrawn at 16x16 in the style of the game's own: small flat palettes, hand-placed highlight and shadow, a dark outline on items, no gradients. Touches every build (the textures are in all ten jars).

## 2026.11.1

First release. The whole progression: scrounging, the pick-hammer chain, panning with five tray tiers, the soaking basin, the crucible, the lure, Nether silt and End silt, and the Void Island world preset. Ten builds: Forge and NeoForge for Minecraft 26.3, 26.2, 26.1.2, 1.21.1 and 1.20.1.
